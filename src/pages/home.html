Walking Form Tips for Balance, Pelvic Alignment, and Knee Safety:
  • Keep the knee of your moving leg aligned with the big toe of your standing/support leg as you step forward.
  • Maintain your feet parallel or slightly inward (mild internal rotation), not turned outward.
  • Walk on the balls of your feet, not the heels, for better shock absorption and stability.
  • Imagine a straight line beneath you and place each foot directly in front of the other along it.
  • P.S: For heavy activities ALWAYS have your supporter. Really Important!
